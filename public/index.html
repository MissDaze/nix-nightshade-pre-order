ABOUT

The phrase came first. It's been living in a notes app for about two years, written during a period that had no shortage of material to draw from. At some point it stopped being a complaint and started being a kind of posture toward things. Wry. Resigned but not broken. Still here.

The design is direct. Black sweatshirt, white type, statement front and centre. The graphic is intentional without being precious. It's meant to be read.

Nix Nightshade is a Melbourne-based independent creator. This is a one-time print run — not a brand, not a shop, not a season. This specific design, on this specific sweatshirt, printed once. The minimum is 60 because the economics don't work below that. The maximum is 150 because scarcity that's actually enforced means something.

The dark humour is the point. If you've had a year that felt like it was personally authored to test your limits — this is for you. You don't need to explain it to anyone.

"This isn't a brand. It's a statement."
