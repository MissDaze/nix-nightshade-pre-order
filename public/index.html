ABOUT

"This isn't a brand. It's a statement."

I designed this because it was either make a shirt or talk to a therapist. The shirt was cheaper.

The phrase has been sitting in my notes app for two years. It started as a complaint and somewhere along the way it became a kind of posture — wry, resigned, still upright. The screw is literal and not. Both at once. That's the point.

I'm Nix Nightshade. I'm based in Melbourne. I make things when making things is the only way to process them.

This is a one-time print run. Not a brand, not a shop, not the start of a collection. This specific design, on this specific sweatshirt, printed once. There's a minimum because the economics don't work below 60. There's a maximum because 150 is enough. Once this window closes, it's closed.

The dark humour is the point. If you've had a year that felt like it had your name on it — this is for you. You don't need to explain it to anyone wearing it.
